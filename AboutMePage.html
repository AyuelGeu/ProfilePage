Experience

My name is Marol Ayuel Geu. I am a South Sudanese

I was born in Kakuma Refugee Camp at Kaopooka Clinics on chilly Wednesday morning on 21st June 2006 to a family of ten
I am the fourth born son of the great Maluat family. I then moved to South Sudan right after my birth.

Educational Background

I started my schooling in South Sudan in 2012 at Nakwathom Heritage Academy. I came to Kenya in 2013 where I had to
again started from Grade 1 at the prestigious Cush Refugee Camp Primary School. I was a dedicated lad since 2014 upto March
2022 where I had built a name for myself, not only in Cush but at the whole location. I was quite known by almost everyone due to
academics excellence and that is where I came to see the importance of Education. It does not only give you skills but also demands
respect for you from other guys. To be sincerely honest, I didn`t want to leave Cush Primary School. It`s a school I admire upto date.
Cush created a lot of memories I will do anything to re-live. I had friends, good friends with dreams and a bright future ahead of them.
I still miss it to date.

I did my Kenya Certificate of Primary Education (KCSE) examination in March 2022 and waited for the results. The results didn`t
dissapoint. I was the Second best in the entirety of Kakuma having got 409 marks out of 500 marks. I applied for scholarsips because
sI didn`t want to study in Kakuma. My dream school was Maranda High School and a little preference to Kapsabet High School.
I was fortunate enough to get Windle International Kenya (WIK) scholarship though they didn`t enrol me in any of my preferred schools.
I was enrolled at Kisii School in the Nyanza Province in Kisii. I was happy regardless because the school was still categorised as a national school.
I started off badly in high school. I don`t know if it was because it was my first time in a boarding school but I think that was one of the reasons.
I fought enough to emerge with a B+ in the end of the four year course during the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education. in Novemver 2025.
I came back to Kakuma and was fortunate to be enrol for a volunteering job at Kakuma Junior and Kindergarten School. There I tried all my best to
equip learners with the skills needed
